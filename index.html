Training rules

  • Most working sets: finish with about 1–3 reps in reserve.
  • Double progression: reach the top of the rep range on all sets, then add a small amount of weight.
  • Use controlled reps and a full comfortable range of motion.
  • Do not add random exercises just to make the workout longer.
  • Optional cardio: 15–25 min easy incline walking or 20–30 min cycling.

Simple chicken cooking

Air fryer

Cut evenly, season, use a little oil, air-fry around 375–400°F and verify the thickest piece reaches 165°F. Time varies by piece size and appliance.

Pressure-cooker curry

Sauté onion + tomato + spices, add chicken and a little water, pressure-cook according to the cooker/manual, then verify 165°F internally. Cool promptly and refrigerate in shallow containers.

Skin rules

  • Introduce one new active at a time.
  • If an active causes persistent burning/redness, stop and simplify.
  • Sun protection is the key daily step for tanning/PIH management.
  • Recurring itchy bumps should be assessed by a clinician/dermatologist rather than assumed to be “body heat.”

Long-term principle

Muscle and strength require continued resistance training to maintain fully. A long break can reduce them, but returning to training is generally easier than starting from zero. No supplement permanently preserves training adaptations.

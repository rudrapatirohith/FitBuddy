Daily nutrition checklist

Simple meal-prep system

Sunday prep
  • Cook a batch of rajma and dal/pappu.
  • Prepare egg curry/bhurji for several meals.
  • Cook rice in measured portions and refrigerate promptly.
  • Portion chicken into 180–200 g raw servings and freeze what will not be used soon.
  • Move the next day's chicken from freezer to refrigerator the night before.
  • Keep chopped onion, tomato, lettuce and other vegetables ready.
Food-scale rules
  • Chicken: 180–200 g raw per main serving.
  • Rice: 150–200 g cooked per serving.
  • Oats: 60 g dry.
  • Paneer: about 100 g when used.
  • Rajma: about 200 g cooked.
  • Dal: about 250 g cooked.
  • Curd: 150–200 g.
  • Nuts: 15–20 g.
  • Granola: 25–30 g; treat it as a topping because it is calorie-dense.
Rice vs oats vs chapati

You do not need to eliminate rice. Oats work well at breakfast, rice works well around lunch/training, and chapati is a convenient dinner carbohydrate. Body-fat change is primarily driven by overall energy balance, not by eliminating one carbohydrate.

Chicken & the "body heat" concern

There is no good evidence that chicken inherently "heats" the body. Since you noticed itchy bumps during a period of frequent chicken meals, the dashboard rotates chicken with eggs, dal and rajma rather than making chicken your only protein source. If a repeatable food reaction occurs, discuss it with a clinician rather than assuming it is simply "heat."

Aesthetics protocol

Clean, Healthy, Athletic

The goal is not extreme dieting or gimmicks. Build muscle, keep body fat controlled, protect skin from UV, maintain good grooming and sleep.

☀️ Morning skincare

1. Gentle cleanse
Use a non-irritating cleanser; don't scrub aggressively.
2. Optional antioxidant/brightening active
Use only an active your skin already tolerates.
3. Moisturizer
Apply enough to keep the skin comfortable and hydrated.
4. Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen
Apply generously and reapply when appropriate for prolonged outdoor exposure.

🌙 Evening skincare

1. Remove sunscreen/impurities
Cleanse gently; avoid repeated harsh washing.
2. One active at a time
Retinoid/BHA/other actives should not be stacked indiscriminately. Introduce slowly.
3. Moisturizer
Prioritize barrier comfort over using more products.

Skin rule: don't chase irritation

More acids, retinoids or products do not automatically mean better skin. If an active causes persistent burning, swelling, severe redness or a worsening rash, stop the offending product and seek professional advice.

🧍 Posture & presence

2-minute posture reset
Chin gently tucked, ribs stacked over pelvis, shoulders relaxed rather than forced backward.
Screen ergonomics

Daily walking

💇 Grooming protocol

  • Hair: Keep the sides/back clean and choose a shape that works with your natural hair texture.
  • Beard: Keep neckline and cheek boundaries intentional; maintain consistent length.
  • Brows: Clean obvious stray hairs without aggressively changing the natural shape.
  • Teeth: Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and clean between teeth daily.
  • Lips: Use a simple moisturizing lip product when dry; SPF lip protection is useful outdoors.
  • Body: Shower after heavy sweating and change out of sweaty clothing.
  • Nails: Keep fingernails/toenails clean and trimmed.
  • Clothing: Proper shoulder fit and clean, well-fitted basics can immediately improve presentation.

When to get skin symptoms checked

Recurrent itchy bumps, hives, persistent peeling, significant swelling, painful lesions or symptoms that repeatedly follow a food/product should be evaluated rather than self-treating indefinitely. Trouble breathing or swelling of the lips/tongue/throat is an emergency.

Weekly check-in

Photo protocol

  1. Take front, side and back photos every 4 weeks.
  2. Use similar lighting, distance, posture and time of day.
  3. Don't flex one week and relax the next.
  4. Compare month-to-month, not day-to-day.

When to adjust calories

If your weekly average weight is falling quickly and gym performance is suffering, increase food modestly. If weight and waist are both rising consistently, reduce portions modestly. If strength is improving and waist is stable or slowly decreasing, leave the plan alone.
